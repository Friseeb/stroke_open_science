## Our data (stroke, 2026-10-13 run)

| Design | Share of articles | Registry number in abstract, 2024 | Own registration in open-access full text, 2022–2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trials | 7% | 35% | 68% (all 520 audited) |
| Systematic reviews | 5% | 22% | 45% (sample of 76) |
| Other studies | 88% | 1.5% | 7% (sample of 137) |

Of registered stroke trials, 57% name ClinicalTrials.gov and 16% ChiCTR; the rest ISRCTN, ANZCTR, NTR, KCT, UMIN, IRCT.
A trial = PubMed trial publication type or a title/abstract that describes one (`apply_study_design_flags`).

None of 14 journals offers Registered Reports, and none is on the Center for Open Science list
(https://www.cos.io/initiatives/registered-reports: "over 300 journals"; 286 regular, 36 special issues; the linked
spreadsheet lists 298). The spreadsheet's one stroke title, "Academia Journal of Stroke", has every feature "TBA"
and no findable web presence (not confirmed).

## Published estimates

- RCTs, all fields: 53% registered, 20% prospectively (Trinquart 2018, BMC Med, doi:10.1186/s12916-018-1168-6);
  41.7% of registered 2018 RCTs prospectively (Al-Durra 2020, BMJ, doi:10.1136/bmj.m982).
- Stroke RCTs: 20% of 600 RCTs from Cochrane reviews had a registry record (many pre-2005; Syed 2023, Eur Stroke J,
  doi:10.1177/23969873231194811); 61.7% of 287 acute ischaemic stroke RCTs in 2022 reviews unregistered, OR 4.87 for
  outcome-reporting bias (Zhang 2025, Cardiovasc Diagn Ther, doi:10.21037/cdt-2025-212); stroke motor rehabilitation:
  46% of 323 registered RCTs unpublished, over 60% registered retrospectively (Aryan 2021, Neurology,
  doi:10.1212/WNL.0000000000011660).
- Other research: registered protocol in 67% of clinical trials vs 3% of other biomedical research (Serghiou 2021,
  PLoS Biol, doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3001107); 10% of 200 observational studies in the top five general journals
  registered, 7% prospectively, 2022 (Leducq 2024, J Clin Epidemiol, doi:10.1016/j.jclinepi.2024.111341); under 1% of
  Swedish registry studies preregistered (Axfors 2024, PMID 38591841, abstract only).
- Preclinical: preclinicaltrials.eu 331 protocols, Animal Study Registry 286 studies (October 2026); "only 550
  preregistered studies worldwide as of 2025" (doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3003905); 10% of 418 Swiss animal study
  directors had ever preregistered, 39% had never heard of it (Priboi 2026, PLoS Biol, doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3003511).

## Policy wording

- ICMJE: "the ICMJE encourages registration of research with non-trial designs, but because the exposure or
  intervention in non-trial research is not dictated by the researchers, the ICMJE does not require it."
- Lancet 2010;375:348 (doi:10.1016/S0140-6736(10)60148-1): investigators "should register observational studies on
  a WHO-compliant registry before they begin" (still cited by Lancet Neurology in 2026).
- JAMA Network Open 2025 (Berlin & Fihn, doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.24181): "We strongly encourage
  registration of observational studies"; about 10,000 cohort studies registered, a small fraction of those published.
- RECORD 2015 asks authors to state whether a protocol exists and whether the study was registered; STROBE 2007 has
  no registration item.
- The debate: against, Vandenbroucke 2010 (doi:10.1097/EDE.0b013e3181e942b8); for, Naudet 2024 BMJ
  (doi:10.1136/bmj-2023-076123); since 2012, 19 of 25 opinion pieces argued for (Malmsiø 2025,
  doi:10.1016/j.jclinepi.2025.111686).
