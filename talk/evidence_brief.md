# Incentives for open science in stroke research: evidence brief

Compiled 2026-10-06 for the WSC 2026 (Seoul, 21-23 Oct) talk. Web-sourced by a research
agent; items marked [unverified] or [secondary] could not be checked against a primary source.
Re-verify any number before it goes on a slide.

## A. Policy landscape, 2026

- **NIH Data Management & Sharing Policy** in force since 25 Jan 2023; approved DMS Plan is a term of award, reported annually in RPPR. NIH says it has evaluated >1,100 plans, most acceptable with minor revision.
- **NIH Public Access Policy (2024)**: zero embargo; effective date pulled forward from 31 Dec 2025 to **1 Jul 2025** (NOT-OD-25-101).
- **NIH APC cap**: 8 Jul 2025 NIH announced limits on allowable publication costs from FY2026; RFI NOT-OD-25-138 floated disallowing all publication costs, a $2,000 per-paper cap, or $3,000 only at journals that pay reviewers and publish reports. Final rule status not confirmed [unverified].
- **US federal reversals**: the 2022 OSTP "Nelson memo" was removed from the OSTP website; OSTP described itself as "in the process of repealing" it (AIP FYI). **EO 14303 "Restoring Gold Standard Science"** (23 May 2025) defines gold-standard science as "reproducible, transparent, communicative of error and uncertainty" and orders agencies to publish data and models behind decisions. Politically contested; cite with care.
- **Canada, Tri-Agency RDM Policy** (Mar 2021): institutional strategies due 1 Mar 2023; DMPs required only for designated opportunities since 2022; the **data-deposit requirement is still not in force** as of 2026.
- **Tri-Agency Open Access Policy**: 2015 policy allows 12-month embargo. Draft revision (consultation Feb-Mar 2025) requires immediate OA, repository deposit, open licence and rights retention, for grants awarded on/after 1 Jan 2026. Final release not confirmed [unverified]. CIHR is moving to a narrative tri-agency CV (Project Grant no sooner than Spring 2027).
- **Wellcome**: CC BY, Europe PMC at publication, data availability statement required; from 1 Jan 2025 APCs paid only in fully-OA (DOAJ) journals. OA.Report found 92% compliance (Aug 2023).
- **UKRI**: immediate OA; data "as open as possible, as closed as necessary"; narrative CV (R4RI) across UKRI funding since Jan 2024 [secondary].
- **Horizon Europe**: immediate OA under CC BY, FAIR data and mandatory DMP. Horizon 2020 OA compliance reached **83%** (EC monitoring report, 2021).
- **Plan S / cOAlition S**: 28 funders; support for transformative arrangements ended Dec 2024; Strategy 2026-2030 (Nov 2025) prioritises diamond OA, preprints and alignment with assessment reform.
- **UNESCO Recommendation on Open Science**: adopted unanimously by 193 member states, Nov 2021; progress reporting every 4 years.
- **ICMJE**: data-sharing statement required for trial manuscripts submitted after 1 Jul 2018; sharing plan in registration for trials enrolling from 1 Jan 2019. The 2016 draft mandating IPD release within 6 months was dropped after pushback (NEJM 2017 editorial).
- **TOP**: >5,000 signatories; TOP Factor scored >3,000 journal policies. **TOP 2025** (Grant et al. 2026, Res Integr Peer Rev) reorganises standards and explicitly targets funders and institutions, not only journals.
- **DORA**: 27,420 signatories in 175 countries. **CoARA**: 797 member organisations at Dec 2025 General Assembly; national chapters 5 (2023) to 19 (2025).
- **Publishers**: Springer Nature making data availability statements mandatory portfolio-wide. **Nature expanded Registered Reports to all disciplines including clinical science on 3 Jun 2026.** Lancet journals require a data-sharing statement for all research Articles.
- Audit of 10 top general medical journals (Vinatier et al., medRxiv Nov 2025): TOP Factor from **1 (NEJM) to 13 (PLOS Medicine)** of 27; only BMC Medicine offers Registered Reports; RCTs 78% stated intent to share data.

## B. Stroke-specific

- **Stroke (AHA)**: all original research must carry a Data Availability Statement at the start of Methods (AHA Author Hub); start date not verified. **IJS**: data availability section required for all original research, repository deposit "encouraged" (SAGE). **ESJ**: follows ICMJE; no stronger wording found. **Lancet Neurology**: statement must say whether, when, where and under what criteria data will be shared. **JAMA Neurology / Neurology**: ICMJE statement. None of Stroke, IJS or ESJ offers Registered Reports (searched; none found).
- **No WSO or ESO position statement on open science or data sharing was found.** ESO Trials Alliance (2019) encourages pre-specified IPD pooling; ESO Health Economics WG is auditing VISTA.
- **VISTA**: >82,000 anonymised patients; 38 acute ischaemic stroke RCTs (2025 paper); steering-committee approval, analysis in Glasgow or remote, project subscription fee.
- **Vivli**: 8,600+ trials, 6.4M participants, 55+ members, 600+ publications (site, Oct 2026). **YODA** (Sci Data 2026): 482 trials; 485 requests, 463 (95.5%) approved; 139 (30%) yielded at least one paper.
- **NINDS**: rt-PA trial data released Oct 2003, about 8 years after publication.
- **ENIGMA Stroke Recovery**: >2,100 patients, 39 studies, 10 countries (Liew 2020). **ATLAS v2.0**: 1,271 T1 MRIs with lesion masks from 44 cohorts, 11 countries, open (Liew 2022, Sci Data).
- **MR CLEAN Registry**: IPD cannot be shared under Dutch law because consent was not obtained; syntax and output available on request. **GWTG-Stroke**: AHA released a 1,000-record synthetic dataset drawn from 7.8M records (Wang 2025, JAHA). **HERMES**: 1,287 patients pooled from 5 thrombectomy trials (Goyal 2016, Lancet): voluntary pooling works when trialists want it.
- **Audits**: Scutt, Woodhouse, Montgomery & Bath 2020 (BMJ Open), vascular and cognitive RCTs: 391 trials; emails found for 313 (80%); 148 (47%) never replied; 87 (28%) declined; **IPD obtained for 78 trials (20%)**; median 241 days to data. Rauh 2020 (Res Integr Peer Rev), 271 empirical neurology papers 2014-18: **9.2% raw data, 0.7% analysis scripts, 3.7% preregistered**. Heckerman 2025 (eLife), 393 cardiovascular papers: **<2%** supply everything needed to reproduce. Montreal Neuro audit (Ebrahimzadeh 2023): 66.5% had a DAS, 94.9% no registered protocol. **No stroke-specific DAS or data-retrieval audit was found.** Our pipeline fills a documented gap.

## C. What incentives actually change behaviour

- **Badges**: Psychological Science open data **<3% to 23% overall, 39% in H1 2015** (Kidwell 2016, PLOS Biol). But the only RCT, at BMJ Open (Rowhani-Farid 2020, 160 articles): **OR 0.9 (0.1-9.0)**, sharing low in both arms. Badges were the only incentive with empirical data in medicine (Rowhani-Farid 2017 systematic review).
- **Mandates**: Cognition's mandatory policy raised DAS 25% to 78% and reusable data 22% to 62%; of 35 reusable datasets, 31% reproduced without help, 31% with help, 37% not (Hardwicke 2018, R Soc Open Sci). ICMJE statement requirement in surgical journals: data obtainable for **2/65 (3.1%) before and 2/65 (3.1%) after** (Bergeat 2022, JAMA Netw Open).
- **Open washing**: of 487 RCTs in JAMA/Lancet/NEJM, 334 declared data sharing, **2 (0.5%)** had downloadable IPD (Danchev 2021, JAMA Netw Open). Of 1,792 authors who promised data "on request", 93% ignored or declined; **123 (6.8%)** shared (Gabelica 2022, J Clin Epidemiol). "Upon request" statements are "inefficient and should not be allowed" (Tedersoo 2021, Sci Data).
- **Registered Reports**: 44% positive results vs 96% in standard literature (Scheel 2021, AMPPS); 29 RRs vs 57 matched papers rated by 353 reviewers scored higher on all 19 criteria, equal novelty (Soderberg 2021, Nat Hum Behav).
- **Citation advantages**: repository-linked DAS gives up to **25.4% (+/-1.1%)** more citations across 531,889 PLOS/BMC articles (Colavizza 2020); 9% (5-13%) for microarray data (Piwowar 2013). OA citation advantage found in 47.8% of 134 studies, in subsets in 23.9%, absent in 27.6% (Langham-Putrow 2021). Only about 20% of 47,593 PLOS ONE DAS pointed to a repository (Federer 2018).
- **Funder compliance**: H2020 83%; Wellcome 92% (2023); NIH about 75% (2012 figure; recent rate not found).
- **Preprints**: 23.6% of 539 COVID RCT papers had preprints; 77% of medRxiv clinical preprints were published within median 6 months, but trials were only 5.9% of pairs (JAMA Netw Open 2023). No robust non-COVID clinical rate found.
- **Careers**: of 92 biomedical promotion guidelines worldwide, **zero** mentioned data sharing, registration or OA (Rice 2020, BMJ); Canadian U15 medicine: 4/5 traditional vs 3/7 progressive criteria present (Rice 2021, FACETS). Moher 2018 (PLOS Biol) six principles. Utrecht dropped the impact factor in all hiring/promotion from 2022 (Nature 2021). Cardiology survey (Cobey 2024, Open Heart, n=198): funding the "most essential incentive"; 54% want practical support; "policy alone will not lead to implementation."
- **Money**: Einstein Foundation Award for Promoting Quality in Research, EUR 500,000/yr since 2021. No experiment paying researchers to share data was found.

## D. Barriers specific to clinical stroke research

- **GDPR**: pseudonymised data remain personal data; head MRI can be re-identified by facial reconstruction, so de-identified imaging "must not be released to the public without further provisions" (Aperture Neuro 2025 editorial; MIDI task group). Consent scope blocks registries outright (MR CLEAN Registry).
- **Industry trials**: ICMJE's 6-month IPD mandate collapsed in 2016-17; controlled-access platforms (Vivli, YODA, VISTA) are the compromise, with fees and remote-analysis constraints (Scutt 2020).
- **LMIC**: LMIC first authors median 4% in major journals; APCs prohibitive, about 3/4 of journals offer no waiver (Frontiers Public Health 2024; PMC10803444). Immediate-OA mandates plus APC caps can shift cost to authors.
- **Korea**: no formal national OA mandate yet; KISTI runs DataON (since 2020) and AccessON; KISTI-UNESCO open-science project runs Mar 2025 to Dec 2029 with a diamond-OA focus; Ministry of Science "considering" a mandate (Shmagun 2024, J Inf Sci; KISTI).

## E. Implementable ideas others have piloted

1. **Verified badges, not self-declared**: TOP 2025's "certification" tier; the BMJ Open null result argues badges need verification to bite.
2. **Registered Reports in stroke journals**: none offer them; BMC Medicine (2017) and Nature (Jun 2026) show the pathway; **funder-journal RR partnerships** (Children's Tumor Foundation + PLOS ONE: in-principle acceptance at grant award) adaptable to WSO/ESO grants.
3. **Data reuse credit**: Make Data Count (DataCite/CDL); Columbia and Calgary count datasets in promotion; "Ten simple rules for recognizing data and software contributions" (PLOS Comput Biol 2024).
4. **Open-science CV items**: UKRI R4RI, CIHR tri-agency narrative CV (2025-27), Utrecht TRIPLE model with open-science fellows per department.
5. **Embargoed sharing**: 12-month post-publication windows already written into COMPASS and ACT-GLOBAL; VISTA as the stroke-specific trusted archive.
6. **Federated or synthetic access**: ENIGMA-style site-level analysis; federated learning for stroke imaging (feasibility papers 2024-26); GWTG-Stroke synthetic dataset.
7. **Funder-journal coordination**: NIH APC cap options reward journals with paid, open peer review; Wellcome refuses hybrid APCs; Plan S strategy ties OA to assessment reform.
8. **Prizes**: WSO reproducibility or reuse award modelled on the Einstein Award.
9. **Ban "available on request"**: Tedersoo 2021 recommendation; Gabelica and Danchev numbers justify it.

## Ten most quotable numbers

1. 6.8% of authors promising data "on request" delivered (Gabelica 2022).
2. 0.5% (2/334) of JAMA/Lancet/NEJM RCTs declaring sharing had downloadable IPD (Danchev 2021).
3. 3.1% to 3.1%: ICMJE statement rule changed nothing in surgery (Bergeat 2022).
4. 20% of 391 vascular/cognitive trials yielded IPD; median 241 days (Scutt 2020).
5. 9.2% raw data, 0.7% code, 3.7% preregistered in neurology (Rauh 2020).
6. 44% vs 96% positive results, Registered Reports vs standard (Scheel 2021).
7. <3% to 39% open data after badges at Psych Science (Kidwell 2016) vs OR 0.9 in the BMJ Open RCT (Rowhani-Farid 2020).
8. 0 of 92 promotion guidelines mention data sharing (Rice 2020).
9. +25% citations for repository-linked data (Colavizza 2020).
10. 82,000 patients in VISTA; 8,600 trials in Vivli; 1,271 open stroke MRIs in ATLAS.
