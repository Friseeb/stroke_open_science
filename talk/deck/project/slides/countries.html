Forty seconds. The ten largest producers plus the Netherlands, first-author country, 2015-2025. The United States, the largest high-income producer, is the least open (58%; 42% closed). The United Kingdom (78%) and the Netherlands (90%) are open mostly by paying: national funders there require and fund open access. Indonesia (95%, upper-middle income) and India (70%, lower-middle) are open mostly through free routes, national free journals. The Netherlands (61%), Germany and China (about half) pay most often. Wealth does not predict it; policy and the journals a community publishes in do.
