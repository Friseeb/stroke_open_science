Finding 2. Most of that openness was bought, not deposited

Route, 2015 to 2025Share
Gold, paid journal25%

Gold and hybrid grew the most. Green, the free route, stayed near 7% for a decade. Diamond is carried by society journals, including the Journal of Stroke from the Korean Stroke Society.

Open-access status from OpenAlex for 126,267 stroke articles with a known status. Bronze articles can be withdrawn from free access at any time.

Open access became a transaction between funders and publishers. It did not come with openness of anything else. For this room: the diamond share is an Asian strength, Korean, Japanese and other society journals carry it. Mention APCs and the LMIC angle in one sentence only.
