Share of stroke articles by OpenAlex status · 2025: counts complete; open-access status still settling

The line to say: open access became a transaction between funders and publishers; it did not come with openness of anything else. Shares 2015 to 2025: gold, paid journal 25%; diamond, free journal 16%; hybrid, paid in a subscription journal 10%; bronze, free to read, no licence 10%; green, repository copy 6%; closed 34%. Gold, diamond and hybrid together are the published-open measure on the previous slide (51% over the decade, 67% in 2025); bronze and green are what free to read adds. From 2015 to 2024 gold grew the most (+18 points); diamond (+9) outgrew hybrid (+7). Green fell from 9% in 2015 to 3% in 2025. Bronze can be withdrawn at any time. For this room: diamond is a long tail of about 4,800 free journals. Cureus is the largest (1,187 articles), then Journal of Physical Therapy Science (487) and Journal of Stroke (334). About a quarter of Korean (27%) and Japanese (25%) first-author articles are diamond; Chinese 5%. Gold plus hybrid is about half of the open articles over the decade (52% with 2025, 49% through 2024) and the majority every year since 2021: 35% in 2015, 53% in 2021, 59% in 2024. One sentence on APCs and the equity angle; the equity slide comes later. The growth figures stop at 2024 because 2025's open-access status is still settling (its article counts are complete). Green fills in last, as repository copies appear after embargoes end, but it was already down to 4% in 2024, so its fall does not rest on the partial year.
