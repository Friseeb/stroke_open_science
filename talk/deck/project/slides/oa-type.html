08  Finding 2

The growth in open access was paid for, not deposited

Route, 2015 to 2025Share
Gold, paid journal25%
Diamond, free journal16%
Hybrid, paid in a subscription journal10%
Bronze, free to read, no licence10%
Green, repository copy6%
Closed34%

From 2015 to 2024 gold, a paid route, grew the most (+18 points); diamond (+9) outgrew hybrid (+7). Green fell from 9% in 2015 to 3% in 2025. Bronze can be withdrawn at any time.

Open access became a transaction between funders and publishers. It did not come with openness of anything else.

For this room: diamond is a long tail of about 4,800 free journals. Cureus is the largest (1,187 articles), then Journal of Physical Therapy Science (487) and Journal of Stroke (334). About a quarter of Korean (27%) and Japanese (25%) first-author articles are diamond; Chinese 5%. Gold plus hybrid is about half of the open articles over the decade (52% with 2025, 49% through 2024) and the majority every year since 2021: 35% in 2015, 53% in 2021, 59% in 2024. One sentence on APCs and the equity angle; the equity slide comes later. The growth figures stop at 2024 because 2025 is still filling in. Green fills in last, as repository copies appear after embargoes end, but it was already down to 4% in 2024, so its fall does not rest on the partial year.
