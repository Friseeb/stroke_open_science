Forty seconds. Open access grew because someone paid. Paid routes (gold and hybrid) went from 19% of all stroke articles in 2015 to 44% in 2024; free routes (diamond and green) from 19% to 24%; bronze, free to read without a licence, fell from 15% to 7%. Of the open articles, 59% paid a fee in 2024 (35% in 2015). Next: who pays.
