07  Finding 2

Most of that openness was paid for, not deposited

Route, 2015 to 2025Share
Gold, paid journal25%
Diamond, free journal15%
Bronze, free to read, no licence10%
Hybrid, paid in a subscription journal9%
Green, repository copy7%
Closed34%

Gold and hybrid, the paid routes, grew the most. Green stayed near 7% for a decade. Bronze can be withdrawn at any time.

Open access became a transaction between funders and publishers. It did not come with openness of anything else.
