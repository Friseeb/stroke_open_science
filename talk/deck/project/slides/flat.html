Thirty-five seconds. Lower bounds from abstracts and metadata, 2024: data in a repository 0.2% (0.2% in 2015, never above 0.6%); code links below 0.1% in every complete year. What did move: registration IDs, 2.8% to 5.3% of articles with an abstract, and preprints, 2.7% in 2024, partly publisher-driven (Research Square, SSRN). Pooled over 2015-2025, high-impact articles are barely better at sharing (data 0.45% vs 0.34%). Then the pivot: so we read the full text. The figure measures detected links, not the availability of all underlying data or code.
