Stroke went open access: 53% in 2015, 75% in 2024

Thirty-five seconds. The good-news slide. Open access = any OpenAlex status other than closed. Stroke sits between cardiology and neurology in every complete year (2015-2024); in the incomplete 2025 it edges past cardiology. Do not rank neuroscience: about 45% of it comes from the title term 'neural', much of it machine learning.
