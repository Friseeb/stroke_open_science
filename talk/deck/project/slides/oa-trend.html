Stroke went open access: 53% in 2015, 75% in 2024

Open access = any OpenAlex status other than closed. Stroke 66% over the decade; cardiology 70%, neurology 62%, neuroscience 65% (its title term “neural” also catches machine-learning papers). 2025 is incomplete.

The good-news slide. Let it land, then turn it on the next one. Stroke sits between cardiology and neurology every year through 2024; in the incomplete 2025 it is just above cardiology (75% vs 74%). The peak so far is 77% in 2023. Do not rank neuroscience: about 45% of it comes only from the title term “neural”, much of it machine learning; without those papers it is 67% open access. It is above stroke until 2019 and below neurology in 2024 and 2025.
