03  Stroke is not immune

Same question, different answers, and no way to tell why from the papers alone

Preclinical

1,026 neuroprotectants tested in animal stroke; none translated. Publication bias overstated efficacy by about a third. NXY-059: SAINT I positive, SAINT II negative.

O’Collins 2006; Sena 2010; Lees 2006; Shuaib 2007

Tirofiban

No benefit before thrombectomy in RESCUE BT (2022). Benefit without a large-vessel occlusion in RESCUE BT2 (2023). A different question, or a different answer?

Qiu 2022, JAMA; Zi 2023, NEJM

Middle meningeal artery embolisation

Three trials in one NEJM issue: EMBOLISE positive (recurrence 4% vs 11%), STEM positive, MAGIC-MT neutral (6.7% vs 9.9%).

Davies, Fiorella, Liu 2024, NEJM

Replication is how a field finds out which result to trust. Without the protocols, the data and the code, we are left arguing about it.

If asked: in the animal work one in seven experiments was never published (Sena 2010). One minute, three examples the room knows. Do not editorialise on any single trial; the point is heterogeneity that cannot be resolved from the papers alone. Confirm that the embolisation trio is the example you meant.
