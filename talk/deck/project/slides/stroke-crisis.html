Fifty seconds, three examples the room knows; do not editorialise on any trial. O'Collins catalogued 1,026 experimental treatments in animal stroke, 114 taken to patients; no neuroprotectant translated (thrombolysis is the exception), and publication bias overstated animal efficacy by about a third (Sena 2010); NXY-059: SAINT I positive, SAINT II negative. Tirofiban: no benefit before thrombectomy in RESCUE BT (JAMA 2022), benefit over aspirin without large or medium vessel occlusion in RESCUE BT2 (NEJM 2023): a different question or a different answer? Middle meningeal artery embolisation for subdural haematoma: EMBOLISE positive (repeat surgery 4.1% vs 11.3%), STEM positive (16% vs 36%), MAGIC-MT neutral (6.7% vs 9.9%, P = 0.10), all in the NEJM in the week of 20 November 2024 (STEM online, print 2025).
