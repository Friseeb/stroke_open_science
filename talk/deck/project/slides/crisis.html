Forty-five seconds. These are my former fields; say so. Psychology: 35 of 97 replications significant (36%), effects half the size (Open Science Collaboration 2015). Neuroscience: median power 21% (Button 2013). Preclinical cancer: Amgen reproduced 6 of 53 landmark findings (Begley 2012). More than 70% of 1,576 researchers had failed to reproduce another scientist's experiment (Baker 2016). Why: four routine analysis choices raise a 5% false-positive rate to 61% (Simmons 2011); 63% of 2,155 psychologists admitted not reporting all their measures (John 2012).
