13  What to do next

A task force, a prize, and awareness

1

A WSO task force on open science

  • A position statement: no stroke society has one
  • Retire “available on request” in Stroke, IJS, ESJ and JSCVD: a repository, VISTA or Vivli, or a stated reason
  • Registered Reports for stroke trials, with in-principle acceptance tied to WSO and ESO grants
  • Embargoed sharing as the trial default, twelve months after publication

2

A prize for the teams that share

  • The commitment index: verified in the full text, recomputed every six months
  • Institutions, journals, funders and countries
  • On the Einstein Foundation model, presented at this congress

3

Awareness

  • The public benchmark, updated every six months
  • Open-science items in promotion criteria: 0 of 92 guidelines today
  • Targets to discuss: data in a repository or controlled-access platform for 25% of stroke articles by 2030; “on request” under 10%; one Registered Report track in a stroke journal by 2028

The statement, retiring “on request” and promotion criteria cost nothing. Registered Reports, the prize and the infrastructure need money, and the infrastructure mostly exists: VISTA, ATLAS, ENIGMA.

Two minutes. Invite the WSO leadership in the room to take the task force home. Say which items cost nothing and which need money.
