A proposal for the WSO

Create a WSO task force.

Reward verified sharing.

Track progress through this benchmark.

One minute forty-five. Proposal: ask WSO to convene a task force and develop a position statement; retire 'available on request' in Stroke, IJS, ESJ and JSCVD in favour of a repository, VISTA or Vivli, or a stated reason; Registered Reports for stroke trials with in-principle acceptance tied to WSO and ESO grants; embargoed sharing as the trial default, twelve months after publication. Prize: the commitment index, verified in full text and recomputed every six months, for institutions, journals, funders and countries. Awareness: the public benchmark; open-science items in promotion criteria (in 2020, 1 of 92 guidelines mentioned data sharing, and none open access or registration). Targets to discuss: data in a repository or controlled-access platform for 25% of stroke articles by 2030 (own repository deposit was 5.9% of 2025 open-access full texts); 'on request' under 10%; one Registered Report track in a stroke journal by 2028. The statement, retiring 'on request' and promotion criteria cost nothing; Registered Reports, the prize and the infrastructure need money, and the infrastructure mostly exists: VISTA and Vivli for trial data, ATLAS and OpenNeuro for imaging, ENIGMA for pooled analysis. Invite the WSO leadership in the room to take the task force home. Proposed targets and deadlines are discussion points, not commitments or current WSO policy.
