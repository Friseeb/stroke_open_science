Open tools shaped my stroke research

Open imaging tools. Reusable lesion datasets.

Sharing enables independent checks.

Fifty seconds, first person. I came to stroke from imaging and neuroscience, where tools such as ITK-SNAP, DeepISLES, SPM Clinical Toolbox, ANTs, VLSM, LESYMAP and BCBtoolkit could be inspected and reused. BIDS and OpenNeuro support sharing. ATLAS v2.0 released 955 public MRIs with lesion masks; ISLES 2022 released 250 cases. These are examples, not a claim that every imaging workflow is open. Liew 2022 found that 12 of 17 ATLAS-based methods did not report public code. Open data alone is not enough. The illustration is conceptual artwork generated with OpenAI Image Generation, not a patient scan or empirical network.
