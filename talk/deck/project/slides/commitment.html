Journals: Nature Communications, Brain Communications, PeerJ, NeuroImage Clinical lead; Stroke is about 15th of 99, at 1.5x. Funders: US federal institutes, ANR, Swiss Heart Foundation, Leducq, DFG. Countries: Belgium 2.4x, Germany 2.2x, Switzerland 2.0x, France 1.8x, UK 1.5x. Index = mean, over data deposited or public source in the statement, GitHub and trial registration in the full text, of the entity's rate divided by the stroke audit rate (4.2%, 1.3%, 14.1%); 1 = stroke average. Institutions: the 60 with the most audited articles (each 82+ in 2015-2025); countries 50+, journals and funders 30+. The current analysis merges Charité and its Center for Stroke Research before calculating the index: 133 audited articles, 4.36 times the stroke average. Oxford has 177 audited articles and Hamburg-Eppendorf 109. This descriptive index averages deposit/public-source, GitHub and registration rates relative to stroke-wide audited rates. It measures recorded practices, with small counts and publication-year composition affecting the order. It is not a validated measure of research quality.
