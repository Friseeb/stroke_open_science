Relative to the stroke average

Forty-five seconds. The index is the mean, over three full-text signals (data deposited or public source, GitHub, trial registration), of an institution's rate divided by the stroke rate; 1 = stroke average. Shown: the top of the 60 institutions with the most audited articles (82 or more each), as on the dashboard. Charité is counted once: OpenAlex lists its Center for Stroke Research Berlin and the Berlin Institute of Health separately, and those are merged into it (133 audited articles; deposit or public source 9.8%, GitHub 10.5%, trial registration 37%). Small counts are a hint, not a ranking. Journals: among the 40 largest on the dashboard, the Journal of Cerebral Blood Flow and Metabolism leads (2.7x) and Stroke is fourth (1.5x); among smaller journals with at least 30 audited articles, Nature Communications, Brain Communications and PeerJ lead. The point: the measurement exists, is cheap, and is what a prize would recognise. Four descriptive examples; sample sizes and full comparison remain on the dashboard.
