11  Who is already doing it

A commitment index, verified in the full text, recomputed every six months

Journals: Brain Communications, PeerJ, Nature Communications, NeuroImage Clinical lead; the big stroke journals are not on the list. Funders: NIH institutes, the ERC, the Center for Stroke Research Berlin. First-author countries: Belgium 2.7×, Ireland 2.1×, Germany 2.0×, UK 1.7×.

Index = mean, over data deposited or public source in the statement, GitHub in the full text and trial registration in the full text, of the entity’s rate divided by the stroke-wide rate (4.3%, 1.4%, 14.9%). 1 = stroke average; minimum 50 audited articles.

Say the n out loud; small counts are a hint, not a ranking. The point is not the names: the measurement exists, is cheap, and is what a prize would recognise.
