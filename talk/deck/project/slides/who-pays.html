Forty-five seconds. This is the story behind 'paid for'. Funded papers are 79% open against 60% without a funder, and 54% of funded papers paid an article fee against 25%. In high-income countries the funder is the switch: 83% open with a funder, 56% without, the least open group of all (29,055 and 44,147 articles); overall, high-income first authors are the least open (67%). In lower-middle-income countries open access does not depend on a funder (74% vs 75%): it comes through free routes. Low-income countries: 400 articles, too few to show. Caveat: 'funder' means a funder listed in OpenAlex, which misses some acknowledgements.
