Funding is associated with open access

Stroke · High-income first authors

Funder listed

83%

No funder listed

56%

Forty-five seconds. Stroke articles, 2015-2025, high-income first-author group: 83% open access when OpenAlex lists a funder (29,055 articles), versus 56% without a listed funder (44,147). This is a descriptive association, not a causal estimate of funding. No listed funder does not prove no funding: acknowledgements may be missing. The groups may differ in year, journal and study type. Source: benchmark 2026-10-13, Income groups.
