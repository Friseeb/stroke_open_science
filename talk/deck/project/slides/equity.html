Among the three larger income groups, lower-middle-income first authors are the most open to read (75%, 42% through diamond or green), yet in open-access full texts, 2015-2025, they write a data statement in 23% (high 43%, upper-middle 57%) and deposit or name a public source in 1.8% (4.0%, 5.2%), from 609 audited articles; the gap holds in 2022-2025. Both panels use the same scale, which is the point: deposit is tiny everywhere. Low-income countries (400 articles, 77 audited) are too few to show. Korea: 5,490 first-author articles, 75% open. This figure shows only deposit/public-source rates, with a 0-6% scale.
