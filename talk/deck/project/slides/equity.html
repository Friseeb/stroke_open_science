10  Who pays to be read, and who shares

Reading access has been solved for the global South by free routes. Reuse has not.

Open-access route by World Bank income group of the first author’s country

What the full text shows, by income group

Lower-middle-income authors publish 42% through routes that cost nothing (high income 19%), yet deposit data in 2.6% (high 4.2%), mention GitHub in 0.1% (1.9%) and a trial registration in 6.8% (15.5%).

Income group known for 86% of the corpus: high 63%, upper-middle 30% (mostly China), lower-middle 6%, low 0.3% (355 articles). Korea: 4,950 first-author articles, 75% open access.

Seoul is a high-income audience with LMIC trialists in the room. The data and code that lower-income teams could build on are not there, and their own work is the least likely to be shared. That is the inequality open science can close.
