Deposit or name a public dataset, 2025

8.8%

4.3% over the decade; authors’ own repository deposit 5.0%

20,515 open-access stroke full texts, 2,000 per year, Europe PMC; optimistic for stroke overall. Elsewhere, “on request” delivers: 6.8% of promising authors shared (Gabelica 2022); 0.5% of top-journal RCTs had downloadable data (Danchev 2021); 20% of 391 vascular and cognitive trials, median 241 days (Scutt 2020).

The slide people photograph. Three numbers, slowly. Against the other fields, 2022 to 2025: stroke writes the most statements (70%) and deposits the least (6.5%; cardiology 10.1%, neuroscience 8.9%). Full text versus abstract for the same articles: GitHub 1.4% vs 0.04%, trial registration 14.9% vs 7.9%.
