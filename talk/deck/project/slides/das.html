Sixty seconds; the slide people photograph. Open-access stroke full texts published in 2025 (the audit reads 1,500 a year, so the year is complete for the audit): 74% write a data statement (14% in 2015), 29% say 'available on request', now the most common answer, and 9.8% deposit data or name a public source (own repository deposit 5.9%). In 2024: 69%, 30% and 7.3%. 'On request' delivers little: 6.8% of authors shared when asked (Gabelica 2022); 20% of 391 vascular and cognitive trials, median 241 days (Scutt 2020). Against the other fields, 2022-2025, stroke says 'on request' most often (30%; neurology 27%, neuroscience 26%, cardiology 24%). Open-access full texts only, so these rates are optimistic for stroke overall. Statement presence overlaps the other indicators; these rows do not partition all statements.
