Sixty seconds. This slide shows a single endpoint, replication, in one project. SCORE directly replicated 151 of 274 claims drawn from social and behavioural science papers published in 2009-2018 (55.1%; Nature 2026). Each mark is one assessed claim; red marks meet the project's primary replication criterion. Outcomes depend on that criterion and this selected sample. This is not a stroke replication rate, nor a before/after trend. The 2015 psychology project found 35 significant replications among 97 tested for significance; its different sample and endpoint cannot establish improvement over time. Statistical power, researcher surveys and data availability measure other constructs and are not pooled here. Tyner et al. doi:10.1038/s41586-025-10078-y.
