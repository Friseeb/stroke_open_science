Fifty seconds, the pivot. The ICMJE data-statement rule left obtainable data at 3.1% in surgical trials (Bergeat 2022). Open-data badges: OR 0.9 in the first randomised trial in medicine, at BMJ Open (Rowhani-Farid 2020), though they took Psychological Science from under 3% to 23% open data, 39% by early 2015 (Kidwell 2016). In 2020, 1 of 92 biomedical promotion guidelines mentioned data sharing, and none open access or registration (Rice 2020). Registered Reports: 44% positive results against 96% (Scheel 2021): changing when review happens changes what is published. Cardiology researchers name funding as the most essential incentive; 'policy alone will not lead to the effective implementation of open science' (Cobey 2024). Stroke researchers are not less honest than psychologists; they respond to the same incentives.
