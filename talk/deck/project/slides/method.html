Forty-five seconds. The method is the credibility. One published run, 13 October 2026: 231,580 OpenAlex records from six title terms, 2015 to 2026, of which 140,281 are stroke journal articles and reviews with a DOI from 2015 to 2025. The full-text audit reads up to 1,500 open-access articles per year in each field through Europe PMC, 16,362 for stroke. Open-access full text only, so those rates run high, and a pooled rate gives every year the same weight, so I lead with single years and say "over the decade" when I pool. Everything that follows is on the dashboard, where the selection can be changed by anyone. On the card, neuroscience leads on code, GitHub, deposit and preprints, partly because its title term "neural", which brings in about 45% of that corpus, also catches machine-learning papers. If asked: neurology 129,644, cardiology 312,703, neuroscience 509,973 articles.
